BOSCO PASTA

PROJECT BRIEF

Develop a distinctive visual identity and packaging system for Bosco Pasta, creating a playful and recognizable brand presence.

STRATEGY

Bosco Pasta combines bold typography, expressive graphics and a warm visual language to create packaging that feels approachable, memorable and contemporary.

IDENTITY

The Bosco Pasta identity uses a flexible graphic system that carries across packaging, printed materials and promotional applications.

APPLICATION

The identity extends across packaging and promotional materials while maintaining a consistent visual language throughout the Bosco Pasta brand.
