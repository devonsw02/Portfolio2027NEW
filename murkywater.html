MURKY WATER

PROJECT BRIEF

Conceptualize and design a business with a consistent brand, products, and signage.

STRATEGY

Murky Water takes a local and modernist approach to cafe branding, combining unique hand-drawn imagery with geometric patterns to create a distinctive visual identity.

IDENTITY

The visual identity combines a restrained modernist structure with expressive, hand-drawn elements. The system creates a balance between repetition, texture and unexpected visual moments.

APPLICATION

The identity extends across packaging, signage and printed materials, allowing the visual language to remain consistent while adapting to different formats and physical environments.
